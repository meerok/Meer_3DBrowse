3DS homebrew

Homebrew means apps and games made by fans rather than by Nintendo. On a 3DS running Luma3DS you can install them as .cia files with FBI or launch them from the Homebrew Launcher.

Getting started

  • Back up your SD card first
  • Follow a trusted setup guide step by step
  • Keep a copy of your NAND backup somewhere safe
Tip: most homebrew stores its settings in the 3ds folder on the SD card.

Popular apps

Emulators, theme managers, cheat tools, save managers and media players are among the most downloaded. Many are open source, so anyone can read the code.
